## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the serene architectural grandeur of South Indian temple heritage—specifically channeling the monolithic stone carving, brass metalwork, and balance of Vijayanagara architectural legacy. Designed for sacred pilgrimage management, the visual tone blends high-trust institutional reliability with the warmth, reverence, and tactile comfort of a boutique heritage retreat.

### Audience & Accessibility
The audience spans multigenerational families, often organized by working adults booking on behalf of elderly parents. Consequently, the user interface demands unambiguous legibility, generous tap targets, calm pacing, and zero visual friction. The mood balances auspicious devotion with logistical clarity.

### Visual Aesthetic
- **Architectural Framing:** Subtly echoing gopuram silhouettes, stone mandapa pillars, and stepped kalyani tanks through proportions and fine ornamentation.
- **Dignified Restraint:** Sacred imagery relies strictly on architectural geometry, deep stone textures, brass oil lamps (diyas), temple kalash silhouettes, and sacred flora (lotus, marigold). No deity faces or idol portraits are featured, preserving universal reverence and serene neutrality.
- **Warm Luminosity:** Avoid cold digital whites and clinical grays. Every neutral is anchored in warm ivory, unpolished sandstone, and burnished metal.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the natural materials and auspicious dyes of traditional South Indian temple complexes: red kumkum powder, hand-hammered brass, crushed turmeric, and age-weathered sandstone.

### Color Roles & Guidelines

- **Primary (`#7A1F2B` - Temple Maroon):** Represents sacred authority, architectural sanctum depth, and heritage. Used for primary interactive actions, high-level headers, active navigation tabs, and critical status badges.
- **Secondary (`#F28C28` - Sacred Marigold):** Emits warmth and festive auspiciousness. Used for high-visibility highlights, special darsan availability badges, countdown indicators, and secondary calls-to-action.
- **Tertiary (`#C9972B` - Antique Brass/Gold):** The material connector. Used for fine hairline dividing rules, ornamental corner brackets, decorative borders, icons, and subtle badge outlines. Never use for small body copy.
- **Neutral Primary (`#3A2E2A` - Smoked Sandstone Brown):** Replaces pure black entirely. Used for body text, form labels, and iconography to maintain warmth and reduce eye strain for senior readers.
- **Canvas Base (`#FFF8EC` - Warm Temple Ivory):** The primary background canvas, reminiscent of handmade raw cotton and silk vastrams.
- **Canvas Surface Layer (`#F3E6CF` - Warm Sandstone):** Secondary background used for nested cards, alternating structural sections, table headers, and form inputs.
- **Utility WhatsApp Accent (`#25D366`):** Dedicated exclusively to immediate pilgrimage helpline interactions and WhatsApp coordination.

## Typography

Typography pairs the regal, high-contrast serif cadence of `Playfair Display` with the utilitarian readability of `Inter`.

### Guiding Principles
- **Elderly-First Accessibility:** The baseline body size never drops below `16px` (`1rem`) in critical user journeys. Line heights remain loose (`1.5` to `1.65`) to ensure effortless reading for devotees tracking itineraries, pickup times, and seva schedules.
- **Editorial Presence:** `Playfair Display` carries the timeless nobility of carved stone dedications. Major headlines use deliberate tracking to maintain classical balance.
- **Label Distinctions:** Subtitles, badges, and operational status markers use `Inter` with elevated font weight (`600`) and slight tracking to avoid misinterpretation of departure times or booking codes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure relies on a harmonious 12-column grid on desktop, scaling to 6 columns on tablet, and a single or dual-column stream on mobile screens.

### Spatial Rhythms
- **Canvas Margins:** Section paddings mirror the open courtyard enclosures of temple mandapams. Content is granted generous negative space rather than compressed data density.
- **Touch-First Distances:** Interactive elements are separated by at least `space-sm` (`12px`) to eliminate accidental presses, especially during mobile booking by older users.
- **Reflow & Responsiveness:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1024px):** Max container width capped at `1280px` centered. Margins standard at `3rem`.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Margins set to `2rem`, gutters at `1.25rem`. Two-column booking summary splits.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Gutter shifts to `1rem`, canvas margins to `1.25rem`. Itinerary timelines and package cards stack vertically into uninterrupted thumb-accessible cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is treated as architectural terracing—inspired by carved granite steps and layered wooden doors—rather than cold synthetic drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Shadow Palette
Every shadow is infused with an amber-sepia tint derived from `#3A2E2A` and `#C9972B`, creating a natural lantern-lit glow rather than an artificial grey smudge.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Sunk):** Applied to base forms and quiet backgrounds (`#F3E6CF`). No elevation. Outlined by hairline antique gold (`rgba(201, 151, 43, 0.2)`).
- **Level 1 (Ground Surface - Cards & Rows):** 
  `0 2px 8px -1px rgba(58, 46, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(201, 151, 43, 0.08)`.
  Paired with an intrinsic gold-tinted hairline border (`1px solid rgba(201, 151, 43, 0.25)`).
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Modals):**
  `0 10px 25px -4px rgba(58, 46, 42, 0.12), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(201, 151, 43, 0.15)`.
  Produces a gentle, warm floating appearance reminiscent of illuminated brass vessels.
- **Level 3 (Fixed Overlays & WhatsApp Floating Action):**
  `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(58, 46, 42, 0.18), 0 6px 14px -3px rgba(122, 31, 43, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a roundedness factor of `2` (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline). This balances the crisp precision of chiseled stone lintels with the soft, welcoming comfort needed for a modern digital service.

### Geometric Nuances
- **Standard Controls (Inputs, Buttons):** `0.5rem` (`8px`) rounded corners preserve structured formality.
- **Cards & Enclosures (`rounded-lg`):** Scaled up to `1rem` (`16px`) to produce gentle, inviting content tiles that hold itinerary details securely.
- **Feature Modules & Banners (`rounded-xl`):** Configured at `1.5rem` (`24px`) to create smooth framing for panoramic temple hill views and sacred destination photography.
- **Architectural Motifs:** Decorative card tops and header dividers may incorporate stylized stepped cresting or subtle gopuram arches using pure CSS masking or SVG hairline dividers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button (Darshan & Booking Flow):**
  - **Height:** Minimum `52px` to ensure effortless thumb interaction.
  - **Background:** Deep Temple Maroon (`#7A1F2B`).
  - **Text:** Warm Ivory (`#FFF8EC`), weight `600`, size `16px`.
  - **Border:** `1px solid rgba(201, 151, 43, 0.4)`.
  - **Hover:** Deepens to `#631620` with an amplified warm amber glow.
- **Secondary Button (Package Details & Inquiry):**
  - **Height:** `50px`.
  - **Background:** Sandstone tint (`#F3E6CF`).
  - **Text:** Temple Maroon (`#7A1F2B`).
  - **Border:** `1px solid #C9972B`.
- **WhatsApp Helpline Action:**
  - **Height:** `52px`.
  - **Background:** `#25D366` with pure white text and WhatsApp chat icon. Exclusively bound to emergency travel help and quick booking inquiries.

### Cards
- **Base Style:** Built on an ivory-to-sandstone surface (`#FFF8EC` to `#F3E6CF`), radius `16px` (`rounded-lg`), elevated at Level 1.
- **Borders:** Subtle antique gold border (`1px solid rgba(201, 151, 43, 0.28)`).
- **Header Accent:** Top edge may feature an inset `2px` marigold (`#F28C28`) or maroon (`#7A1F2B`) indicator bar denoting booking status or tier level.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Darsan Slots & Categories:** Height `38px`, horizontal padding `16px`, radius `8px`.
- **Default State:** Background `#F3E6CF`, text `#3A2E2A`, hairline border `#C9972B/30`.
- **Selected State:** Background `#7A1F2B`, text `#FFF8EC`, border `#7A1F2B`.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Dimensions:** Minimum height `50px` for text inputs and date pickers.
- **Field Styling:** Background `#FFF8EC`, border `1.5px solid rgba(58, 46, 42, 0.2)`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#7A1F2B` with a `3px` focus ring in `rgba(242, 140, 40, 0.25)`.
- **Labels:** Positioned above the field in `15px` weight `600` `#3A2E2A` for absolute legibility.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Size:** `22px x 22px` for comfortable targeting.
- **Coloration:** Unchecked border `2px solid #C9972B`. Checked state fills with `#7A1F2B` showing a crisp ivory tick or center disc.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Pilgrimage Day-by-Day Timeline:** Stepped vertical tracker featuring brass diya icons as checkpoint nodes, linked by a dotted antique gold trace line.
- **Seva Availability Badge:** Compact pill tag featuring a Marigold yellow fill (`#F28C28`) with deep brown text (`#3A2E2A`) indicating live tokens remaining.
- **Traditional Kolam Section Dividers:** Ultra-thin vector flourishes used sparingly between major page sections to reinforce the consecrated heritage motif without visual noise.
