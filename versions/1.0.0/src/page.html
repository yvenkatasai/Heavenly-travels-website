    A LITTLE CLARITY

    Before
    you set off.

    Simple answers to help you plan with confidence.

    Can I change a package’s temples?+

    Yes. Choose a package, then tick or untick temples in “Customize your stops”. You can also build your own route. Our team will check the route and travel times with you.

    What does the starting estimate mean?+

    The displayed estimate combines the selected vehicle’s starting base with ₹249 for each selected temple stop. Distance, travel days, tolls, permits and other requirements can change the final cost. You’ll receive a confirmed quote before booking.

    Are darshan tickets included or guaranteed?+

    Darshan ticket booking assistance is subject to official availability. Selecting a tour does not reserve a ticket or guarantee entry. Please discuss your requirements with our team.

    Which vehicle is right for our group?+

    Choose from Sedan, SUV, Tempo Traveller or Mini bus / Bus. If you are unsure, select “Help me choose a vehicle”. Share your group size, luggage and comfort needs; our team will confirm the model and suitable seats.

    Can I ask for a quote without choosing everything?+

    Of course. Leave your tour or vehicle undecided, and tick “I haven’t decided my date yet” if needed. Add your name and pickup location, then ask for help on WhatsApp or call us.

    When is my trip actually booked?+

    This website prepares your enquiry. Opening WhatsApp does not book or charge anything. Our team will discuss the itinerary, availability, price and booking arrangements with you.
