Source of visual direction: user-supplied `../../../reference/screen.png`, `../../../reference/code.html` and `../../../reference/DESIGN.md`.

Sticky compact branded navigation, atmospheric full-height architectural hero, warm factual assurance strip, centered carousel cards with scale/depth transitions, a second vehicle carousel, and one unified quote/itinerary panel. ## Current interaction revision

Original conceptual hill hero restored with ambient movement and scroll zoom. Package and vehicle selectors use stacked cyclic stages with faded neighbors and side arrows. Vehicle selection stays on its stage with a scroll-to-review cue. Cart tiles reuse the selected package image and representative vehicle illustration. Inline selection dialogs preserve fields; route checkboxes support per-package remembered edits for the page session. On phones, both compact cart tiles appear side by side above the route builder.

## Hero and plain-language revision

Large serif Heavenly Tours & Travels name at the top of the hero; removed header logo and left brand. Right navigation retains section-aware shine. Hill scene uses a 160svh scroll stage (150svh mobile), sticky viewport, transform-only scroll zoom and copy movement, layered gradient mist and sun rays, static SVG grain and six small decorative particles (three on mobile).
