# Case study draft: making Heavenly's trip information easier to verify

Prepared 23 November 2026. Draft only; not published to LinkedIn or Instagram.

Heavenly Tours & Travels is a Tirupati / Renigunta travel agency owned by Hemanth Kumar. Customers choose temple trips, vehicles and accommodation preferences, then discuss the arrangements on WhatsApp.

Before this work, the site focused on interactive trip selection. Several trip cards and FAQ answers were assembled by JavaScript, and there was no dedicated factual About page or public static catalog with a page for every trip.

The improvement adds nineteen indexable content pages including the homepage, thirteen trip detail pages, an About page, booking information, questions and privacy information. The seven individual homepage trip cards and eight FAQ answers are now built into HTML. A generated JSON catalog and plain-text discovery files provide the same business and trip facts to readers and automated tools.

Booking boundaries matter: there are no public prices or instant reservations. Hemanth Kumar handles payment, confirmation, cancellations, refunds and date changes on WhatsApp. Tirumala ticket support is guidance only. Structured data describes trips and services without fictitious stock, ratings or checkout claims.

Checks cover TypeScript, the production build, canonical links, local assets, all thirteen trip IDs and schema itineraries, noindex vehicle selection, security headers, redirects and real HTTP 404 responses. Browser checks cover readable mobile pages, the existing FAQ and package selection flow.

This demonstrates implemented technical readiness for discovery and comparison. It does not establish that ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity recommends the business. Independent reputation evidence and multi-platform blind-test results remain to be collected. No courses or certificates were completed on behalf of the owner, and no autonomous payment protocol was deployed.
